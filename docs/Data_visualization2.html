Answers to your questions I

  • More info about the cows - We do not know where the cows are..somewhere in Norway. For this reason it is not possible to get weather data.

  • Battery voltage - battery_voltage is the voltage between the terminals of the battery. It is a function of the battery’s charge (decreases with decreasing charge). The unit associated with the data is cV: 340 cV empty battery, 420 cV full battery.

  • Solar charge - unit “milliampere-hours” (if divided by 226 it you get it in cV). solar_charge indicates how much the solar panel has charged the battery in the last 30 minutes. For battery_voltage above 410 cV, charging from is turned off. For battery_voltage below 405 cV, charging is turned on again.

Answers to your questions II

  • Step count - Just a measure of how active the cows are. It should not be interpreted as an actual pedometer, and increases with other types of activity as well, as long as the animal moves its head violently enough.

  • Time

    • time \(\rightarrow\) time when the message was stored on the collar
    • server_time time when the message was received by the server.

The time intervals between seq messages will be longer than 30 minutes if the cloven does not have good enough mobile coverage to send - in which case the messages are sent only when the animal moves into an area with good enough coverage

  • Firmware update - Happens regularly, not so important.
