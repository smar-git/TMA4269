# Project 1

## Part 2: Modelling traffic load

You have delivered a pipeline for modelling of traffic load. When
defining that pipeline, you have made several choices. You will probably
have to make more choices, and you might have to go back and change some
previous choices. Make sure that all of this is recorded in your daily
reflection sheets.


For part 2 of the project you are working towards **a presentation of 10
slides**. Our proposed format is as follows:

-   Slides 1-3: Data visualization and descriptions (figures, tables)

-   Slide 4: Pipeline / flowchart showing data wrangling and
    preparations for modelling.

-   Slide 5: *Reflections:* What is the goal with the data analysis, and
    how is the final goal related to your thoughts in Part 1

-   Slides 6-8: Model formulation, results and interpretation

-   Slide 9: A (tabloid) newspaper headline, with a figure and a brief
    statement, presenting a key finding from your model

-   Slide 10: A timeline of the project work which documents you
    progress. Piece together the reflection notes and draw a timeline
    addressing the main aspects from Part 1 and 2.

*You can decide to use somewhat more/less slides per theme but you have
to answer to all points above within 10 slides.*

**Note:**

The presentation should be coherent, this includes the visualizations.

The presentation will be evaluated against your Part 1 hand-in, so make
sure to document and argue for major deviations from your initial plan.

*Not part of the final evaluation, but an extra 5 points earned on the
first Friday following the project deadline (October 9th): 5-10 minutes presentations
(3 groups together).*
